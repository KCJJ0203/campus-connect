# Publicity Plan

## Proposed Activities
- **Digital Posters (2 weeks prior):** Create digital promotional posters featuring a universal Call to Action (CTA) and QR code for campus noticeboards and monitors.
- **Social Media Countdown (1 week prior):** Launch a daily countdown campaign on Instagram and TikTok to build excitement leading up to the event.
- **Information Booth (3 days prior):** Set up an interactive info booth in the student lounge with a **QR code banner** allowing passersby to instantly scan and complete free sign-ups.
- **Flyer Distribution (2 days prior):** Design flyer templates with the event CTA and registration link/QR code to distribute during peak campus hours.
- **Club Announcements (1 week prior):** Coordinate with student clubs to share event announcements, posters, and sign-up links across their official group chats.
