# Publicity Plan

## Proposed Activities
- Create digital promotional posters for campus noticeboards and monitors.
- Launch a social media countdown campaign to build excitement.
- Set up an information booth in the student lounge to answer questions.
- Design flyer templates to distribute during peak campus hours.
- Coordinate with student clubs to share event announcements in group chats.
